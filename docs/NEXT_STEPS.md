## Done in the hotfix after iteration 37 (the night crash)

- Reported by the player: tabs crashed shortly after the first roads and zones. Cause: the
  first dusk. Every glowing particle was drawn as a light with a continuous intensity, and
  the light-sprite cache created and kept a canvas element per distinct key, over twenty
  thousand in one night. Fixed by quantizing the particle intensity (eleven steps), bounding
  the cache, and a Playwright night scene (`scripts/playtest37.mjs`) that counts canvas
  elements (96 across a run that made 20,000 before). Also found: the autosave carried the
  walkers' transient bonus layer as 80,000 plain-JSON numbers; it is skipped now and dropped
  from older saves on load (252 KB instead of about 2 MB).

  - **Folk:** the light-first rule costs Vellish seeds 42 and 44 a third and half of their folk
    against iteration 54 (1,942 and 1,280 at day 150, from 3,060 and 2,739). They hold their
    zoning through brownouts the old bot zoned through dark. Seed 44 ends in a 0.28 brownout with
    two Fonts.
  - **Loans:** seed 43's Beacon and the Tallowkin's Lantern of Wicks both ride on the Crown's loan,
    with 70,000 still owed at day 150.
  - **Breadth:** only the Veshari bot stands a cradle (and keeps a tide drake) and a depot in the
    final table. Walls stand only on Vellish 43. Breeding never happens: it needs two grown dragons
    of two lines in one attuned cradle.
  - **Variance:** the six runs swing a lot between passes. A one-line change to the bot sends
    every later roll down another path, so a single seed per people is a thin measure. A wider
    table (three seeds a people) would show the bot's levers better than any one run.
- **The bot's hunt** (iterations 58 and 61). The bot's lodges stand on hounds in every run, so its hunt
  crowns are small (0–517 a run) and come from hound lodges switched to game between packs. Since
  iteration 61 ten of twelve bot runs stand a depot (DESIGN §4.92).
- **The Tallowkin bot's depot** (iterations 61 and 63). On iteration 61's seed it stood none: its Wick-Towers read the
  treasury and spent it first. Since iteration 63 the bot values the caravan, and the Tallowkin stand a depot in 6 runs
  of 6 (median day 37).
- **No bot Weir in the runs** (iteration 61). No bot hut fishes its shoal under half, and no five fords
  lie in one Weir's reach on the six seeds; the rule is held by tests only.
- **Head-on paintings and a road behind.** Since iteration 59 every building turns toward its road
  by a mirror. The painted zone houses drawn head-on (most level-2 hearths, the craft levels, the
  arcane level 2) keep their front to the viewer, so on a road behind them (their upper edges) they
  still show their front to the empty side. Only new paintings, three-quarter views of the same
  houses, would turn them.
- **The bot's runs are noisy** (iterations 60, 62 and 64). One extra roll on day 10 moves a seed by up to half its
  folk (σ within a seed: Vellish about 430, Tallowkin about 520). The wide bar (DESIGN §4.95) sees a tenth for the
  Grimmen and the Veshari, and 10.2 % and 11.3 % for the Vellish and the Tallowkin at its cap of 8 draws. A full
  comparison takes about 2¼ hours: commit first, and rerun after a stop to resume from the cache.
- **The Grimmen fall in the Ember** (seen in iterations 54 and 55). The Grimmen bot loses a third
  of its folk between days 60 and 70 (964 to 665 in iteration 55, 923 to 628 in 54). No home is
  abandoned, mana demand falls from 340 to 218 and happiness dips to 51. Something in the Ember
  (the season's craft or harvest rules, or the hill halls) empties homes without a message. Worth
  a probe of its own.

- **The flood's price.** With the Hush flood, Veshari seed 44 reaches the Weirhold on day
  82 instead of 55 and ends 220 folk smaller; the bot only answers the flood with bargemen
  and with the Weir at Vesh's Court. A cheaper flood-work (a bank wall, a sluice) or a bot
  that keeps hearths off the first bank row would close the gap.

- **Software-rendered performance.** Under headless Chromium with SwiftShader a 500-building,
  800-agent city renders in ~10 ms at 2–3× zoom but ~28 ms at 1× (the full-resolution blit
  and light pass dominate in software). On a GPU-backed browser this is well under the
  16 ms budget, but 1× zoom on a very large city should be re-measured on real hardware
  and, if needed, the light map dropped to quarter resolution at 1×.
- **Problem icons** wait two days and hide at 1× zoom; the Districts view now carries a
  per-district unlit / dry / empty summary under each label.
- **The bot player is a test tool**, not a demo: it zones aggressively and sometimes leaves
  water gaps that a human would fix in seconds.
- **Agents walk on the tile grid**, so citizens occasionally cut a corner visually where a
  road turns. Restless that lose their road hop to the nearest one.
- **Cross-chunk depth**: props are cached per ground chunk and depth-sorted per frame, but a
  very tall tree directly behind a tall building on a chunk boundary can overlap by a pixel
  or two.
- **Mana** is still the late-game budget: 4–6 nodes, deepened twice, plus Thrum Fonts and
  Torrent Mills carry roughly 3,000–4,000 folk. Past that the answer is the daughter
  charter on another node.
- **Undo** covers player actions only; a building the Gloam or a fire removed is not undoable.
- **A replay begins with a new charter** (iteration 67). A charter loaded from a save, or imported, records nothing, since
  a rebuilt save is not the live state bit for bit. A replay from another build may diverge, and the game warns when it
  loads one.
- The GitHub Pages workflow deploys from `main` only; a branch preview would need a second
  job.
