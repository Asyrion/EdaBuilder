# Eda City Lore — canon vs. proposed

This file separates what the repository already established about Eda from what
*Wardlight* adds, so canon and new ideas can be told apart at a glance.

## Given (found in the repo / brief when work started)

- The world is called **Eda**.
- It is medieval, high fantasy with a dark-fantasy undertone.
- Magic is central: arcane power, magical artifacts, potions and alchemy.
- A sister game, **Ossuary**, is set in Eda and focuses on potions and alchemy.
- Nothing else. The repository contained only a one-line README.

## Proposed (invented for Wardlight) — all of the following

Everything below is new and is marked *proposed*. It never contradicts the given
material; where the given material is silent it invents.

### World structure *(proposed)*
- Ley ("thrum") runs in Veins under the earth and surfaces at **ley nodes**.
- The continent **Orrin**; the kingdom **the Ashen Crown of Vell**; its capital
  **Vell-on-Sable**.
- The **Aurel Concord** magocracy, its **Great Conduit**, and its fall in the
  **Hollowing** three centuries ago.
- The present age, the **Long Dusk**; the blighted wilds called the **Gloam**.
- The **Charter** system and the player's title, **Warden-Reeve**.

### Peoples *(proposed)*
- **Hearthfolk**, **Tallowkin**, **Grimmen** (see `EDA_LORE.md` §3).

### Religion *(proposed)*
- **The Four Vigils**: Aume, Vesh, Morrow, Ilka. The Aurel as a fallen, remembered god.

### Magic rules *(proposed)*
- Ley must be channelled through leystone; overdraw causes wild surges.
- Unquieted dead become restless; deathcare is a public service.
- The Gloam creeps over unwarded land; Ward Lanterns hold it back.
- Alchemy and enchanting as the two magical industries.

### Creatures and wyrms *(proposed)*
- Glim-born, Gloam-born, Concord-made, sky-born and Vein-born creatures as listed in
  `EDA_LORE.md` §8; Vein-Wyrms as Eda's dragons; drakes as their bred cousins; rare
  reagents (glim-dust, rayscale, wyrm-scale). Wyrmcraft: five dragon lines from eggs,
  cradles, tamers and the Wyrm-Ring. Design mechanics in `DESIGN.md` §8.

### Relationship to *Ossuary* *(proposed, deliberately loose)*
Wardlight treats alchemy as Vell's premier trade and names its advanced deathcare
building the **Ossuary**. It makes no claims about *Ossuary*'s plot, characters or
specific potions, so that game remains free to define them. If *Ossuary* later
establishes contradicting canon, the Wardlight names in `src/sim/catalog.ts` and the
flavor strings in `src/data/flavor.ts` are the only places that need to change.

### The city you found *(proposed — used in play)*

**The Charter of the Hollow Vale.** The Crown has surveyed a young ley node in a
river valley at the edge of the Gloam and named it worth a wardlight. Your Charter
gives you a purse of crowns, a surveyor's map and one instruction: *"Light it, hold
it, and make it pay."* The valley has no name yet; the player names it (the game
offers Vellish-sounding suggestions such as *Thornwick*, *Ashmere*, *Glimford*,
*Hollowmoor*, *Candlecross*).

Milestone titles the Crown bestows as the wardlight grows *(proposed)*:
Founding Stake → Hamlet → Thorp → Village → Township → Market Town → Burgh →
Free City → Beacon of Eda.

### Naming conventions *(proposed)*
- Places: Old-English/Vellish compounds (-wick, -mere, -ford, -moor, -cross, -holm).
- Tallowkin names: short, with a doubled consonant (Pell, Tammet, Wick, Ossa).
- Grimmen names: soft and grey (Ashe, Morrow-born, Hollis, Sable).
- Hearthfolk names: plain (Bram, Edda, Tobin, Maud, Corin).
